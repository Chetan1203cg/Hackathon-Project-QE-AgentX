# Client Pitch

## Executive Message
QE AgentX modernizes SDLC/STLC quality operations by giving your teams one AI-native control tower for test assets, traceability, reporting, and release readiness.

## Situation
Today, your quality process depends on disconnected channels:
- Jira comments
- Excel sheets
- Confluence updates
- PDF evidence
- Email summaries

## Pain Points
- Slow reporting and sign-off
- Manual RTM and audit preparation
- Weak story-to-defect visibility
- Low historical reuse and growing test debt

## Action
QE AgentX introduces centralized lifecycle intelligence:
- Requirement and risk analysis
- Test design automation
- RTM and coverage automation
- Sprint trend and defect analytics
- Readiness recommendation

## Inputs
- Jira Story/Task
- Acceptance Criteria
- Sprint and release context
- Execution and automation outcomes

## Outputs
- Test cases and regression candidates
- RTM
- Coverage and trend insights
- GO / GO WITH RISKS / NO GO recommendation

## Business Result
- 8-12 hours of release-quality prep reduced to 15-30 minutes
- Better decision quality for PO, QA lead, and release manager

## Adoption Path
- Start with SQLite MVP
- Scale to PostgreSQL for enterprise rollout
