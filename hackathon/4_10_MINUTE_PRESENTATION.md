# 10-Minute Presentation

## Slide 1: Title
QE AgentX MVP: AI-Native QE Control Tower for SDLC/STLC Excellence

## Slide 2: Situation (Today)
No dedicated TMS in use. Teams rely on Jira + Excel + Confluence + PDFs + Email.

## Slide 3: Process Reality
Release validation, RTM, reporting, and sign-off are mostly manual and repeated every sprint.

## Slide 4: Pain Points
- No centralized quality dashboard
- Manual traceability maintenance
- Weak trend visibility (pass/fail/defects)
- Duplicate test assets and test debt growth

## Slide 5: Strategic Repositioning
QE AgentX is not only AI test generation. It is a Quality Engineering Control Tower.

## Slide 6: 8-Agent Architecture
1. Requirement Intelligence
2. Test Design
3. Knowledge
4. Traceability
5. Automation Intelligence
6. Test Debt
7. Sprint Intelligence
8. Release Readiness

## Slide 7: Future-State Flow
Jira Story -> QE AgentX -> Test Cases -> RTM -> Knowledge Store -> Trend Analytics -> Defect Insights -> Readiness Decision

## Slide 8: Output Layer
- Test assets and regression repository
- RTM and coverage views
- Execution and defect trend analytics
- Recommendation: GO / GO WITH RISKS / NO GO

## Slide 9: Business Value
Manual release-quality effort: 8-12 hours
Target with QE AgentX: 15-30 minutes

## Slide 10: Call to Action
Run a controlled 2-sprint MVP pilot with KPI tracking for cycle time, traceability completeness, coverage confidence, and defect leakage trend.
