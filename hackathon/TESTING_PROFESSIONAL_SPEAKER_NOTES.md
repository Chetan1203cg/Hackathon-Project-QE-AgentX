## 🎯 DELIVERY TIPS

### **Pacing (12 Minutes Total)**
- Slide 1 (Opening): 60 seconds — Hook hard
- Slide 2-3 (Problem): 150 seconds — Build empathy, establish stakes
- Slide 4-6 (Solution): 180 seconds — How it works, workflow, HITL
- Slide 7-9 (Value): 210 seconds — Strategic alignment, benefits, ROI
- Slide 10 (Outcomes): 90 seconds — Recap metrics
- Slide 11 (Vision): 75 seconds — Paint the future
- Slide 12 (CTA): 120 seconds — Pilot roadmap, closing ask

### **Energy & Modulation**
- Start high energy (slide 1-2)
- Dip slightly for technical detail (slide 5-6)
- Rise again for value & ROI (slide 7-10)
- Peak for vision & CTA (slide 11-12)

### **Audience Engagement**
- Slides 2-3: "How many of you see this?" (pause for hands)
- Slide 5: Technical credibility—speak with confidence
- Slide 10: Ask for commitment ("Are you ready to lead?")

### **Visual Focus**
- Don't read text on slides—elaborate verbally
- Use gestures to guide attention
- Maintain eye contact 70% of time
- Move across stage to stay dynamic

### **Handling Tough Crowds**
- CFO skeptics: Lead with 3:1 ROI, payback metrics
- CTO concerns: Emphasize HITL gates, compliance, integration
- QA leadership concerns: Highlight team empowerment, not replacement
- Innovation judges: Lead with 60× metric, competitive advantage story
