## 💡 SPEAKER TIPS

### **Opening Energy (Critical)**
- Stand center stage for Slide 1
- Use dramatic pause after "60× faster"
- Make eye contact with 3+ audience segments
- Speak with conviction—not tentative

### **Building Credibility (Slides 5-6)**
- Use technical terminology confidently (Gherkin, Xray JSON, RTM)
- Explain 8-agent orchestration clearly—not rushed
- Emphasize human-in-the-loop gates multiple times
- Reference testing best practices by name

### **Closing Strong (Slide 12)**
- Make the ask specific: "90-day pilot, one product line"
- Paint the future state: "AI-powered autonomous QE"
- End with a question: "Are you ready to lead the transformation?"
- Pause for effect—let it resonate

### **Handling Objections**
| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "Our testing is too complex for AI" | "Complexity is where we excel. If ACs can be written, we can design tests for them." |
| "Will this replace our QA team?" | "No. It redeploys 40% of design capacity to higher-value execution and quality analysis." |
| "How do we know it actually works?" | "90-day pilot. Your baseline is 120 min/story. We target <15 min total. Measure against your data." |
| "What about compliance?" | "HITL gates. Full audit trails. 100% traceability. Designed for regulated industries." |
